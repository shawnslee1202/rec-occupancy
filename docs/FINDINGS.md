# Weight-room findings through November 1, 2026

The analysis covers October 22 through November 1. The collector completed 36 successful polls on nine UTC dates and recorded 1,181 distinct readings across 71 zones.

## Count freshness

At first capture, the median reading was 106 minutes old. Nine in ten readings were at most 329 minutes old. This distribution includes closed and inactive zones.

## Opening hours

Only 83 readings passed the quality and opening-hours checks. Usable counts covered 40.3 of 157.5 scheduled hours at Southside, 26.4 of 92.5 hours at Polo Road, and 44.7 of 154.5 hours at Main. A reading represents at most 90 minutes and stops earlier if another observation arrives.

## Crowding

No covered interval exceeded 90% of capacity at any weight room. Missing intervals could contain peaks, so the available readings cannot identify which room spent the most time crowded.

## Forecasts

None of the three rooms had the 42 days of eligible readings needed for evaluation. Forecast accuracy and changes across the semester remain unmeasured.
